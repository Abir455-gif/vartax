Tax Services

Core Compliance Services

These are foundational services designed to help clients meet their statutory tax obligations accurately and on time.

Tax Return Preparation & Filing

Preparation and submission of annual corporate and individual income tax returns, ensuring accuracy and timely filing before Tax Day.

Tax Deduction at Source (TDS) Management

Ensuring compliance with withholding tax obligations on payments such as salaries, contracts, and service fees. This includes calculating, deducting, depositing TDS, and filing quarterly returns to avoid penalties.

Advance Tax & Minimum Tax Monitoring

Calculating and advising on advance tax and minimum tax payments required quarterly for many businesses, helping clients manage cash flow and avoid interest charges.

Strategic Advisory & Tax Planning

These services focus on minimizing tax liabilities and structuring business operations efficiently.

Tax-Efficient Business Structuring

Advising on the most beneficial business entity, such as a private company or publicly traded company, and structure to optimize the applicable corporate tax rate under the Act.

Cross-Border & International Tax Structuring

Advising on the tax implications of inbound and outbound investments, foreign remittances such as dividends, royalties, and fees, and leveraging Double Taxation Avoidance Agreements to minimize tax leakage.

Transfer Pricing Documentation

Reviewing cross-border related-party transactions, preparing transfer pricing reports, and conducting benchmarking studies to align with arm's length principles and prevent assessment risks from NBR.

Expatriate Tax Advisory

Providing tax planning and return preparation for foreign nationals, including advice on residency status, applicable tax treaties, and tax equalization arrangements.

Tax Health Checks & Due Diligence

Conducting comprehensive reviews to identify tax risks, compliance gaps, or opportunities. This is particularly valuable for mergers, acquisitions, or investments.

Dispute Resolution & Representation

Services designed to represent and support clients during audits or disputes with tax authorities.

Audit & Assessment Representation

Representing clients during income tax audits and assessments initiated by the Deputy Commissioner of Taxes. This includes preparing responses to NBR queries and defending tax positions.

Appeals, Tribunal & Litigation Support

Handling appeals against adverse assessment orders before the Commissioner of Taxes (Appeals), the Taxes Appellate Tribunal, and, if necessary, the High Court Division.

Alternative Dispute Resolution (ADR)

Advising on and representing clients in the ADR mechanism, which is available as an alternative to prolonged litigation to achieve a binding settlement.

Specialized Advisory

TIN & Tax Return Services

Assisting clients with obtaining a TIN certificate, submitting tax returns, and receiving the official acknowledgement of submission.

Tax Clearance & Residency Certificates

Assisting clients in obtaining necessary tax clearance certificates and tax residency certificates for various purposes, such as remittances or treaty applications.
